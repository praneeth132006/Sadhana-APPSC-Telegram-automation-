Privacy Policy

Last updated

1. Summary

We store the minimum needed to give you what you paid for: your Telegram ID, which pass you bought, and when it ends. We never see or store your card, UPI or bank details — those go directly to Razorpay. We do not sell your data to anyone.

2. What we collect

  • Telegram ID and username. Telegram gives us these when you message our bot. We need your ID to add you to the group and to know when your access ends.
  • Your name, if set on Telegram. Used only to address you in messages.
  • Payment records. The amount, the pass you bought, the date, and Razorpay's payment reference. We keep these as our record of sale and for tax purposes.
  • Contact details you send us. If you email us about a refund, we keep that correspondence.

3. What we never collect

Card numbers, CVV, UPI PIN, bank login details, or any other payment credential. These are entered on Razorpay's own secure checkout and are never transmitted to or stored by us.

4. Why we hold it

  • To grant, extend and end your access to the private group.
  • To send you your invite link and remind you before a pass expires.
  • To answer questions and process refunds.
  • To keep records required by Indian tax and accounting law.

5. Where it is stored

Subscriber and payment records are stored in a private Google Sheet accessible only to us, reached over an authenticated connection. Access to our administrative tools requires a verified sign-in and is restricted to named curators.

6. Who we share it with

We share data only with the services needed to run this, and only what each one needs:

  • Razorpay — to take payments and issue refunds. Governed by Razorpay's own privacy policy.
  • Telegram — to message you and manage group membership. Governed by Telegram's privacy policy.
  • Google — for the private spreadsheet holding our records.

We do not sell, rent or trade your data. We disclose it otherwise only where the law requires it.

7. How long we keep it

Membership records are kept while your pass is active and for a reasonable period afterwards so you can rejoin or query a payment. Payment records are kept for as long as tax law requires, currently eight years. You can ask us to delete anything not legally required.

8. Your rights

You may ask us to:

  • Tell you what data we hold about you.
  • Correct anything that is wrong.
  • Delete your data, where we are not legally required to keep it.
  • Stop messaging you — though this ends the service you paid for.

Email and we will respond within 7 working days.

9. Children

This service is intended for candidates preparing for a competitive examination and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their data.

10. Cookies

These public pages set no advertising or tracking cookies. Our administrative dashboards, which are not open to the public, use a sign-in session cookie purely to keep a curator signed in.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed, and meaningful changes will be announced in the group.
