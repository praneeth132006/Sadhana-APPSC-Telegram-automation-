Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated

1. What you are buying

A pass gives you access to a private Telegram group for a stated period. Access begins immediately after a successful payment, when the bot sends you a single-use invite link. Because access starts at once and the content is delivered continuously, a pass is a digital service that is consumed as it runs.

2. Cancelling an auto-renewing subscription

The Monthly Auto-Pay pass renews itself every 30 days until you cancel it. You can cancel at any time by sending /cancel to our Telegram bot.

Cancelling stops all future charges. It does not cut your access short — you keep the group until the period you have already paid for ends. You are never charged again after you cancel.

The one-time passes (30-Day Sprint and Target APPSC 2026) do not renew, so there is nothing to cancel. They simply end on their expiry date.

3. Refunds

We refund in full, to the original payment method, in these situations:

  • You were charged but never received access. If a payment succeeded and no invite link reached you, and we cannot restore your access, we refund the full amount.
  • You were charged more than once for the same pass. The duplicate charge is refunded in full.
  • You were charged after cancelling. Any charge taken after a confirmed cancellation is refunded in full.
  • You request a refund within 24 hours of your first purchase and have not used the group in a substantial way. This applies to your first purchase only.

4. When we cannot refund

  • Requests made more than 24 hours after purchase, where access was delivered and worked.
  • A period that has already been used — for example, asking in week three of a 30-day pass.
  • Removal from the group for breaking the Terms & Conditions, such as sharing your invite link or reposting our content.
  • Dissatisfaction with exam results. We provide practice material; we do not guarantee any outcome.

5. How to request a refund

Email from the address you paid with, or message us on the phone number below. Include your Telegram username and the approximate date and amount of the payment so we can find it.

We respond within 1 working day. Approved refunds are submitted to Razorpay immediately and typically reach your account within 5–7 working days, depending on your bank. The exact timing is set by your bank, not by us.
